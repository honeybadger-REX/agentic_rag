Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

For purchases made through the mobile application, refunds must
be requested through the app's billing section.

Digital products that have already been fully consumed are not
eligible for a refund.

Refund requests are normally processed within 5-7 business days.

Enterprise customers have a separate refund agreement defined
in their contract.

Enterprise customers should refer to their contract for the
specific refund terms applicable to their organization.
